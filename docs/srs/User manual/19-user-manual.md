## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 About SDTS

The Student Digital Twin System (SDTS) is your university's academic intelligence platform. It creates and maintains a live digital twin — a virtual profile — of every student, giving each person a personalized view of their academic progress, learning patterns, skills, and career readiness.

**What SDTS does for you:**
- **Students** — See your live GPA trend, engagement score, and personalized academic recommendations.
- **Teachers** — Quickly mark attendance and enter grades for your classes.
- **Advisors** — Monitor at-risk students early and access their complete academic profile.
- **Administrators** — Manage users, courses, enrollment, and generate institution-wide reports.

### 1.2 System Requirements

SDTS is a web application. You need only a modern browser:

| Browser | Minimum Version |
|---|---|
| Google Chrome | 110+ |
| Mozilla Firefox | 108+ |
| Microsoft Edge | 110+ |
| Safari | 16+ |

No installation or plugins required.

### 1.3 Accessing SDTS

Open your browser and go to:

```
https://sdts.university.edu
```

You will see the login page. Contact your System Administrator if you have not received your login credentials.

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Logging In

1. Open `https://sdts.university.edu` in your browser.
2. Enter your university **email address** and **password**.
3. Click **Sign In**.
4. You will be directed to your role-specific dashboard.

> **Note:** After 5 incorrect password attempts, your account will be locked for 30 minutes. Contact your administrator if you are locked out.

### 2.2 First Login — Change Your Password

If this is your first login (or your administrator gave you a temporary password):

1. Click your name or avatar in the top-right corner.
2. Select **Change Password**.
3. Enter your current (temporary) password.
4. Enter and confirm your new password.
   - Minimum 8 characters
   - Must contain: uppercase, lowercase, a number, a special character
5. Click **Update Password**.

### 2.3 Navigating the Interface

The SDTS interface has three main areas:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Header: User name, role, notifications bell │
├────────────────┬────────────────────────────┤
│                │                             │
│  Left sidebar  │   Main content area        │
│  (navigation   │   (changes per page)        │
│   menu)        │                             │
│                │                             │
└────────────────┴────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Sidebar** — Click any menu item to navigate. Your current page is highlighted.
- **Header** — Shows your name and role. The bell icon (🔔) shows unread notifications.
- **Logout** — Click your name in the header → **Logout**, or use the sidebar logout button.

## 3. Student Guide

### 3.1 Your Dashboard

When you log in as a student, you see your personal dashboard. It shows:
- **GPA Summary** — Current CGPA and this semester's GPA.
- **Attendance Overview** — Average attendance rate across your enrolled courses.
- **AI Insights** — Current at-risk status and top recommendation.
- **Recent Activity** — Latest grade updates and notifications.

### 3.2 Viewing Your Digital Twin

Your **Digital Twin** is a complete virtual picture of your academic life. It has five sections:

1. **Academic Profile** — GPA trend over time (chart), credits earned, enrollment history.
2. **Learning Behavior** — Engagement score, attendance rate, assignment submission rate.
3. **Skills Profile** — Competency levels by domain (Programming, Math, Communication, etc.) compared to expected levels.
4. **Career Readiness** — How well your current profile matches your declared career goal.
5. **Performance Forecast** — AI-predicted GPA for this semester and your at-risk classification.

**To view your Digital Twin:**
1. Click **My Digital Twin** in the sidebar.
2. Use the tabs to switch between the five sections.
3. Hover over any chart to see detailed data.

> **Note:** AI forecasts are generated by a machine learning model and are for guidance only — not an official academic assessment.

### 3.3 Viewing Your Grades

1. Click **My Grades** in the sidebar.
2. Select a semester from the dropdown to view that semester's grades.
3. The table shows: course name, teacher, grade letter, grade points, credit hours.
4. Semester GPA and CGPA are shown at the bottom.

### 3.4 Viewing Your Attendance

1. Click **My Attendance** in the sidebar.
2. Each row shows a course with: total sessions, present, absent, late, excused, and attendance %.
3. Courses with attendance below 75% are highlighted in orange.

### 3.5 Viewing Recommendations

1. Click **Recommendations** in the sidebar.
2. Recommendations are organized by type: Course, Study Habit, Career, Academic Support.
3. Click a recommendation to see the full rationale.
4. Use the **Helpful / Not Helpful** buttons to give feedback.
5. Click **Dismiss** to hide a recommendation you do not wish to see again this semester.

### 3.6 Setting Your Career Goal

1. Click **Career Profile** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Edit Career Goal**.
3. Select or type your target career (e.g., "Software Engineer", "Data Analyst").
4. Click **Save**.
5. The system will update your Career Readiness Score based on your current skills vs. the requirements for that career.

## 4. Teacher Guide

### 4.1 Teacher Dashboard

Your dashboard shows:
- **My Courses** — Courses you are teaching this semester with enrollment counts.
- **Upcoming Sessions** — Next scheduled sessions.
- **At-Risk Alerts** — Students in your classes who are flagged as high risk.

### 4.2 Marking Attendance

1. Click **Attendance** in the sidebar.
2. Select the **Course** from the dropdown.
3. The date defaults to today. Change it if marking for a past session.
4. A list of enrolled students appears.
5. Click the status button next to each student:
   - **P** = Present (green)
   - **A** = Absent (red)
   - **L** = Late (orange)
   - **E** = Excused (blue)
6. Click **Save Attendance**.

> **Note:** If you have already marked attendance for this date and course, saving will update the existing records (upsert — no duplicate created).

### 4.3 Entering Grades

1. Click **Grades** in the sidebar.
2. Select the **Course Offering**.
3. A list of enrolled students is shown.
4. Select the grade letter for each student from the dropdown (A, A-, B+, B, …, F).
5. Grade points are auto-filled based on your selection.
6. Click **Save Grades** to save as draft (students cannot see yet).
7. When ready, click **Finalize Grades** to publish (students are notified; grade can only be changed by admin after finalization).

> **Important:** You can only finalize grades for a course you are assigned to teach. Finalized grades are permanent and visible to students.

### 4.4 Viewing Class Performance

1. Click **Class Performance** in the sidebar.
2. Select a course.
3. See: grade distribution chart, attendance summary, and a list of students with their current status.
4. Students flagged as "High Risk" have a red badge.

## 5. Academic Advisor Guide

### 5.1 Advisor Dashboard

Your dashboard shows:
- **At-Risk Students** — A prioritized list of your assigned students with High or Medium risk flags.
- **Recent Alerts** — Newest notifications (new high-risk flags, attendance drops).
- **Advising Overview** — Count of students by status.

### 5.2 Viewing the At-Risk Student List

1. Click **At-Risk Students** in the sidebar.
2. The list shows: student name, risk level (HIGH / MEDIUM), at-risk score, and key contributing factors.
3. Use the filter to show only HIGH or MEDIUM risk students.
4. Sort by Risk Score (descending) to prioritize your meetings.
5. Click a student's name to open their full profile.

### 5.3 Viewing a Student's Digital Twin

1. From the at-risk list (or **My Students** list), click a student's name.
2. You see their full digital twin — all five sections.
3. The **Performance Forecast** section shows the top 2–3 factors contributing to their at-risk score.

### 5.4 Adding Advising Notes

1. Open a student's profile.
2. Click **Add Advising Note**.
3. Enter the date of the session and your notes.
4. Click **Save Note**.
5. Notes are visible to advisors and system administrators only — not to the student.

### 5.5 Viewing All Assigned Students

1. Click **My Students** in the sidebar.
2. See all your assigned students with CGPA, at-risk level, and last advising date.
3. Search by name or student ID.

## 6. Department Administrator Guide

### 6.1 Managing Courses

1. Click **Courses** in the sidebar.
2. **Add Course:** Click **New Course**, fill in name, code, credit hours, department, prerequisites.
3. **Create Course Offering:** Select a semester and teacher. Click **Create Offering**.
4. **Edit / Deactivate Course:** Click the course row → Edit.

### 6.2 Managing Students

1. Click **Students** in the sidebar.
2. **Add Student:** Click **New Student**. Fill in personal details, assign a program and advisor.
3. **Enroll Student:** Open a student's profile → **Enrollments** tab → **Add Enrollment** → select course offering.
4. **Withdraw Student:** From the enrollment list, click **Withdraw** next to the course.

### 6.3 Managing Teachers

1. Click **Teachers** in the sidebar.
2. **Add Teacher:** Click **New Teacher**. Fill in details and assign to a department.
3. **Assign to Course:** Go to the course offering and assign the teacher.

### 6.4 Generating Reports

1. Click **Reports** in the sidebar.
2. Select report type:
   - **Semester Progress Report** — All students in your department for the current semester.
   - **At-Risk Report** — Students currently flagged as High or Medium risk.
   - **Department Performance Report** — Aggregate statistics for the department.
3. Select semester and format (PDF or Excel).
4. Click **Generate Report**.
5. A loading message appears. When the report is ready (usually < 30 seconds), click **Download**.

## 7. System Administrator Guide

### 7.1 User Management

1. Click **Admin** → **Users** in the sidebar.
2. **Create User:** Click **New User**. Select role (STUDENT, TEACHER, ADVISOR, DEPT_ADMIN, MANAGEMENT, SYS_ADMIN).
3. **Deactivate User:** Click the user row → **Deactivate Account**. The user cannot log in but their data is preserved.
4. **Reset Password:** Click **Reset Password** to set a temporary password; the user is prompted to change it on next login.
5. **Unlock Account:** If a user is locked out after 5 failed attempts, click **Unlock Account**.

### 7.2 System Configuration

1. Click **Admin** → **Settings**.
2. Configurable options include:
   - Attendance alert threshold (default: 75%)
   - At-risk score thresholds (High: 70, Medium: 40)
   - Active semester
   - Notification preferences

### 7.3 Viewing Audit Logs

1. Click **Admin** → **Audit Logs**.
2. Filter by: user, action type, date range.
3. Audit logs are read-only and cannot be deleted.

### 7.4 AI Model Management

1. Click **Admin** → **AI Settings**.
2. View current model versions and last training dates.
3. Click **Trigger Retraining** at the start of each new semester.
4. View model performance metrics (MAE, F1).
5. If the AI service is unavailable, toggle **Rule-Based Fallback** on/off.

## 9. Notifications

SDTS sends notifications to keep you informed of important events.

| Notification | Sent To | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Attendance below 75% | Student, Advisor | Course attendance drops below threshold |
| New grade posted | Student | Teacher finalizes a grade |
| High at-risk flag | Advisor | Student's at-risk score ≥ 70 |
| Account locked | User | 5 consecutive failed login attempts |

**Viewing notifications:**
1. Click the bell icon (🔔) in the header.
2. A panel shows your unread notifications.
3. Click a notification to go to the relevant page.
4. Click **Mark all as read** to clear the badge count.

## 11. Troubleshooting

### "Invalid email or password"
- Check that Caps Lock is off.
- Use the exact email address you were given (usually your university email).
- If you have forgotten your password, contact your System Administrator.

### "Account is locked"
- Your account is temporarily locked after 5 failed login attempts.
- Wait 30 minutes and try again, or contact your System Administrator to unlock immediately.

### Page loads but shows "Error loading data"
- Refresh the page (F5 / Ctrl+R).
- If the error persists, log out, close the browser, and log in again.
- If it still persists, contact your System Administrator.

### My attendance shows 0% even though I attended class
- Attendance is entered by your teacher. If you believe there is an error, speak to your teacher.
- If the teacher confirms they entered attendance correctly, contact your Department Administrator.

### My GPA appears incorrect
- Only finalized grades are included in the GPA calculation.
- If a grade is still showing as "Draft", the teacher has not finalized it yet.

### "At-risk predictions are showing as estimated"
- The AI prediction service may be temporarily unavailable.
- Rule-based estimates are shown instead. The AI service will automatically resume; all predictions will be updated when it does.

### Dashboard is loading slowly
- Check your internet connection.
- Try a different browser.
- If slowness persists for many users, contact the System Administrator (possible server performance issue).

## 12. Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| CGPA | Cumulative Grade Point Average — your overall average across all semesters |
| GPA | Semester Grade Point Average — average for one semester only |
| Digital Twin | Your live virtual profile in SDTS — updates with every academic event |
| At-Risk Score | AI-computed 0–100 score indicating probability of academic difficulty |
| Engagement Score | 0–100 metric reflecting your participation (attendance, submissions, logins) |
| Career Readiness Score | 0–100 match between your current skills and your declared career goal |
| Recommendation | A personalized AI-generated suggestion for courses, habits, or career steps |
| Finalized Grade | A grade that has been officially published by the teacher — cannot be changed without admin approval |
| Enrollment | Your registration in a specific course offering for a semester |
| Course Offering | A specific course taught by a specific teacher in a specific semester |
| Soft Delete | When a user or record is deactivated — it is not deleted from the database, only made inactive |
